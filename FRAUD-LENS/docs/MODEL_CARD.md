# FraudLens Model Card

## Purpose

FraudLens produces advisory risk scores and human-review findings for synthetic financial transaction data. It is intended for demonstration, testing, education, and internal decision support. It is not intended to authorize transactions, freeze accounts, determine guilt, or replace regulated fraud controls.

## Data

- Default dataset: deterministic synthetic CSV generated by `generate_synthetic_dataset`
- Default scale: 8 accounts and 360 transactions over 30 days
- Labels: synthetic boolean `is_fraud_label`, present only for demonstration
- No real personal data, real account numbers, real payment credentials, or live bank feeds are used by default

## Features

- Log amount and amount z-score relative to the organization baseline
- Hour and night-time flag
- Transaction counts in the previous one-hour and twenty-four-hour windows
- Whether counterparty, device, and location were seen earlier in the window
- New counterparty and new device flags
- Encoded transaction type, channel, and status

## Models

- Rule engine: explainable thresholds for amount, velocity, novelty, location, and time of day
- Isolation Forest: unsupervised anomaly score
- Random Forest or XGBoost: supervised probability when at least two positive and two negative labeled examples are available
- Composite score: weighted combination of rule, supervised, and anomaly signals, clamped to 0-100

## Evaluation

Stored metrics include accuracy, precision, recall, F1-score, ROC-AUC when available, confusion matrix, sample count, and positive sample count. Reports expose these metrics alongside feature importance.

## Explainability

Each risk score stores feature contributions and reasons. The global explanation endpoint aggregates contributions and reports the algorithm, metrics, finding counts, high-risk count, and documented limitations. Transaction-level explanations are available by transaction ID.

## Thresholds

- Medium risk: 50-74
- High risk: 75-89, configurable with `FRAUDLENS_HIGH_RISK_THRESHOLD`
- Critical risk: 90-100

## Limitations

- Synthetic labels are easier than real-world fraud labels and may overstate performance.
- Fraud patterns shift quickly, so continuous review and retraining are required.
- False positives can delay legitimate activity; false negatives can allow fraud to proceed.
- Graph clusters show related activity, not proof of coordination or wrongdoing.
- Model output must never automatically block funds, close accounts, or make accusations.
- Human investigators must review evidence and record a resolution before closing cases.
